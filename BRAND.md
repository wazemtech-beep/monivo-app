# Monivo

A fictional connected business-workspace identity applied to the existing Avelety
Next.js application. Layouts, photography, interface illustrations, fonts and
motion timings are preserved. Internal `avelety` names remain implementation APIs.

## Identity

- Charcoal #1A1D20
- Coral #A94132
- Apricot #F1B99F
- Aqua #87C4C5
- Pale gold #F2D689

Copy, metadata and contact details: `src/data/siteConfig.ts`. Main colour tokens:
`src/styles/tokens.css`. Header and footer wordmarks: `BrandLogo.tsx`.

All accounts, balances, customer quotes, security statements, product features,
prices and outcomes are illustrative demonstration content. Monivo is not a live
bank, payment provider or production SaaS service. Email hello@monivo.example and
phone +1 (202) 555-0186 are fictional placeholders. Forms are frontend-only and
indexing stays disabled. The original design attribution is retained.

## Generated Logo

Built-in ImageGen asset: `public/brand/monivo-mark.png`.

Prompt: Original fintech software symbol for MONIVO. A compact friendly M formed
from one continuous folded ledger strip, two gently rounded rising arches and
clean negative space. Apricot #F1B99F with a restrained aqua #87C4C5 inner edge.
Legible at 32px, flat crisp graphic, tightly framed, transparent background.
No words, stock currency symbols, mockups, variations, gradients, shadows or
textures. Paired with editable live text in `BrandLogo.tsx`.
